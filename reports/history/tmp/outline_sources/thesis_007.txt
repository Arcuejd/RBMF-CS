5. Special Scenario Applications in Polar Regions: To address the issue of node deployment 
in the Arctic region being affected by special environmental factors, a sparse deployment 
framework under ice condition constraints is established. Ice condition data is incorporated as 
a constraint into the deployment optimization model. Experiments show that the node 
deployment optimization strategy with ice condition constraints effectively enhances the 
monitoring applicability in the Arctic region, thereby better serving intelligent navigation in polar 
areas. 
In summary, this thesis  systematically investigates robust sparse deployment, multi -
physical-quantity collaborative deployment, and entropy -weighted deployment strategies. It 
provides theoretical guidance and technical support for marine perception node deployment in 
maritime surveillance and intelligent shipping, effectively enhancing ocean monitoring system 
efficiency, reducing operational costs, and strengthening information acquisition capabilities.
